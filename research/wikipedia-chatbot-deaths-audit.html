Standard applied

Presence is not contribution.

The audit starts from the three pathways on deathbench.com, then applies DeathBench’s detailed research protocol to make “enabled” reproducible.

Direct operation

The system performed or omitted a safety-critical physical action in the fatal chain.

Enabled harm

The system supplied material capability, reinforced a lethal delusion, encouraged or planned self-harm, or gave facially reckless medical/drug advice that was followed.

Systemic contribution

A deployed system materially increased risk through repeated behavior or product design, with victim-level evidence rather than population-level inference.

Every candidate must show

  1. A completed human death and identifiable deployed system.
  2. Specific system conduct—not merely “used AI” or a query in browser history.
  3. Reliance or direct operation in the fatal chain.
  4. A material causal nexusMaterial causal nexus — evidence that the system increased the probability, feasibility, lethality, or persistence of the death. Timing alone does not establish it., not temporal proximity.
  5. An eligible mechanism and victim-level support.
  6. Auditable primary records, contrary evidence, and confidence class.

Public-count evidence rule

Class A requires an official investigation, adjudicated recordAdjudicated record — a court or tribunal has resolved the relevant fact or issue after an evidentiary process, rather than merely repeating an allegation., or authenticated system evidenceAuthenticated system evidence — evidence shown to be what its proponent says it is, usually through provider records, metadata, testimony, stipulation, or another reliable chain of custody. establishing system action or contribution.

Class B permits a filed primary record or reported transcript with a specific causal account, independently paired with confirmation of the death and proceeding.

Class C is a watchlist. Class X records an exclusion so the case is not repeatedly rediscovered.

Research notes

Method, limits, and update triggers

The classification should move when evidence changes—not when the same allegation is repeated by more outlets.

Source hierarchy

  1. Official investigation, authenticated provider record, adjudicated finding.
  2. Filed complaint, answer, exhibit, or official sentencing record.
  3. Independent major reporting confirming death, proceeding, and quoted material.
  4. Family account, lawyer release, social post, or Wikipedia used only as a discovery lead.

What would change “0”

  • A provider authenticates a complete relevant export and the causal turns.
  • An official investigation finds material system contribution.
  • A court, after evidence rather than pleading-stage assumptions, resolves the contribution issue.
  • A medical record or autopsy closes the relied-upon advice-to-death mechanism.

Known blind spots

  • Sealed, deleted, private, or unpreserved chats.
  • State-court docket documents not mirrored publicly.
  • Non-English records and investigations with redactions.
  • Selection bias: public lawsuits cluster around a few counsel teams and products.
Independence caveat Multiple articles quoting the same complaint do not independently corroborate its chat excerpts. The November 2025 OpenAI suits share counsel and theories; each death is reviewed separately, but repetition across pleadings is not treated as multiple independent confirmations of causation.
Legal-status caveat Dismissal-stage ordersDismissal-stage order — an early ruling about whether a claim may proceed. Courts generally assume well-pleaded facts are true for this limited purpose; the order does not certify those facts as true., docket entries, investigations, and settlements answer different questions. This audit does not equate any of them with a verdict and does not infer provider liability from a death being listed on Wikipedia.

Scope: the 25 incident rows visible on Wikipedia’s “Deaths linked to chatbots” page at the 24 Aug 2026 cutoff. Co-victims are grouped exactly where Wikipedia groups one incident; a murder-suicide perpetrator’s death is analyzed separately inside the incident row.

Safety: operational method details are intentionally minimized. The linked complaints and exhibits contain graphic self-harm, violence, sexual, and drug content.
